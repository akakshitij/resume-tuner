# The fit score (0–1)

Use when someone asks "should I apply?" or brings several postings at once.

| Part | Weight | What to compare |
|---|---|---|
| Title and level | 0.35 | Their titles and years against the posting's title and its verbs ("support" < "own" < "lead") |
| Skills | 0.35 | Required skills weigh more than nice-to-haves. Count Present and Buried, not Missing |
| Domain | 0.15 | Same industry or product type (fintech, B2B SaaS, consumer mobile…) |
| Logistics | 0.15 | Location, remote/hybrid, contract type, right to work if the posting states it |

Score = sum of (part score 0–1 × weight). Two decimals.

- **Under 0.55: skip**, unless there's a warm route in (a referral, a hiring manager they know).
- For every score give **why they match** (two lines) and **what a recruiter will question**
  (two lines). Honest both ways.
- When comparing several postings, end by naming the one or two to act on today, and why.
