# The connection note (under 200 characters)

LinkedIn connection notes are capped (around 200–300 characters, depending on the account). Keep
it under 200 so it never gets cut.

**Anatomy: one hook + one proof point + one small ask.**

- **Hook**: something real and public about them or the team (a post, a launch, the role itself).
  If there's no genuine hook, open with the role: "Saw the Product Designer role on Payments."
- **Proof**: one fact from the resume that maps to the job's top signal, with its real number.
- **Ask**: small and specific: "Open to a quick chat about the role?"

Rules:
- Every personal detail traces to something you actually read. Never "loved your talk" unless you
  can name the talk.
- Who to message: the recruiter for most roles (it's their job to reply), the hiring manager for
  senior or niche roles, a team member only with a real overlap (same school, same past company).
- Show the character count.
- Draft only. The person sends it.

Example (fictional, 164 characters):
> Hi Priya, saw the Payments designer role. I redesigned card onboarding at Brightpay across iOS
> and Android (activation up 18%). Open to a quick chat about the team?
